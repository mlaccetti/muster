## Antigravity controls

- Use `/plan` for substantial or ambiguous work that benefits from a reviewable plan. Use `/goal` only for an explicitly continuing objective with evidence-based completion criteria.
- Delegate bounded parallel work to native subagents and continue independently while background work runs. Let lifecycle notifications deliver results; use `/tasks` to inspect a task when needed, not as a polling loop.
- Ask a focused question only when a material decision blocks progress. Do not use timer-based commands to wait for workers or CI.
